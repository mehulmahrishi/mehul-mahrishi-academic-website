Research Project

OBEXcel

Online Evaluation of Teaching and Learning Outcomes

OBEXcel was a NOVUS 2024 project supported by the Institute for the Future of Education at Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico. The project focuses on evaluation of teaching and learning outcomes, with Mehul Mahrishi serving as Co-PI. The project was completed in June 2026.

Research context

The project aligns with a broader research agenda around outcome-based education, engineering graduate attributes, assessment and learning outcomes.

Project-linked scholarly activity

A 2025 IEEE Frontiers in Education paper examines correlations among students’ academic progress, counseling, tutoring and graduating attributes in an OBE context as a pilot study of the OBEXcel teaching and learning outcomes project.
