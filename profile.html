Biography

Mehul Mahrishi has 17+ years of teaching and research experience and currently serves as Professor & Head of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan, Jaipur. He previously worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Writing Lab, Institute for the Future of Education, Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico. His research focuses on educational technology and innovation, Artificial Intelligence in higher education, Natural Language Processing and sentiment analysis.
