Biography

Mehul Mahrishi is a Professor and Head in Computer Science & Engineering with more than 17 years of teaching and research experience. His research spans artificial intelligence in higher education, educational innovation, outcome-based education, natural language processing, sentiment analysis and applied deep learning. He previously worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for the Future of Education, Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico.
